## What Doorstop Does

Doorstop builds operational identity workflows for Google Workspace environments.

The platform focuses on the operational work that IT and security teams routinely perform but that existing identity systems often leave fragmented across admin consoles, spreadsheets, tickets, and manual procedures.

Current product areas include:

- Employee and contractor offboarding
- OAuth and third-party application review
- Access review workflows
- Workspace user visibility
- Audit-focused operational records
- Identity lifecycle operations

Doorstop is intentionally narrow in scope. The platform currently focuses on Google Workspace environments and operational identity workflows rather than broad IAM replacement.

---

## Principles

We build around a small set of operational principles:

- Read-only by default where possible
- Explicit review before destructive actions
- Auditability over automation theater
- Operational clarity over AI hype
- Narrow, reliable workflows over broad platform claims
- Human approval for state-changing operations

The goal is operationally trustworthy software — not opaque automation.

---

## Argus

Argus is the internal analysis and prioritization layer inside Doorstop.

Argus may:

- Score OAuth grants
- Prioritize review queues
- Surface operational context
- Assist with workflow analysis

Argus does **not** autonomously execute destructive actions.

Operators remain in control of approvals and state-changing workflows.

## Current Focus

Doorstop is currently focused on:

- Google Workspace identity operations
- Offboarding workflows
- OAuth/application review
- Access review systems
- Operational auditability
- Tenant-scoped operational workflows

Doorstop is **not** currently positioned as:

- A general-purpose IAM replacement
- A broad identity governance suite
- An endpoint security platform
- A generic “AI security” product

The product is intentionally constrained.

## Engineering Philosophy

We prefer:

- Observable systems
- Deterministic workflows
- Operational traceability
- Explicit ownership boundaries
- Infrastructure-oriented product design
- Incremental operational maturity

We avoid:

- Unnecessary abstraction
- Automation theater
- “Magic” operational behavior
- Speculative AI workflows
- Broad platform claims unsupported by the product
